### CHEM-000501: Uniformly reliable surface hopping
- **Subfield:** Molecular reaction dynamics and spectroscopy
- **Open:** Develop an affordable trajectory method treating coherence, decoherence, state crossings and equilibrium populations consistently across coupling regimes, with controlled errors against quantum dynamics.
- **Status:** Surface hopping has developed over decades. Mannouch and Richardson’s 2023 mapping approach supplied a principled alternative, while general reliability for realistic multistate dynamics remains unresolved.
- **Type:** theory | engineering
- **Why:** Photochemical branching depends on electronic transitions and nuclear motion evolving together.
- **Key works:** J. R. Mannouch and J. O. Richardson (2023), *A mapping approach to surface hopping*, [arXiv:2212.11773](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.11773).
